🔒 Cybersecurity Basics

Learn how to stay safe online

What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, and data from attacks, damage, or unauthorized access.

Common Threats

  • Phishing: Fake emails or messages that trick you into sharing information.
  • Malware: Harmful software such as viruses and ransomware.
  • Weak Passwords: Easy-to-guess passwords that attackers can crack.

How to Stay Safe

  • Use strong, unique passwords for every account.
  • Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA).
  • Keep your software and devices updated.
  • Don't click on suspicious links or attachments.
  • Back up your important files regularly.
